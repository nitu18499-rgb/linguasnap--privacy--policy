Privacy Policy – LinguaSnap

Effective date: 6 November 2026

LinguaSnap (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a language-learning and educational app. It offers language-learning content, a Daily Editorial Hub, profile customisation, and learning-streak features. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

1. Information and app activity

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • The display name or profile name you enter.
  • Your learning progress, daily streak, and app preferences.
  • Technical information needed to operate, maintain, and protect the app.

LinguaSnap does not currently require Google or email sign-in to access its main features. Information you enter in the app may still be saved so that your profile and progress can be available when you reopen it.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to display and personalise your profile, save learning progress and streaks, provide app features and content, maintain app security, and improve the app.

3. Storage and service providers

Depending on the feature and app configuration, information may be stored on your device and/or processed by backend service providers, such as Supabase. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not claim that every item is stored in every location.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

Google and its advertising partners may process device information and advertising identifiers as described in Google's policies and your device or advertising settings. Ad availability and personalisation may depend on applicable settings and requirements.

Google advertising help: https://support.google.com/admob/

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when needed to operate app features, provide advertising if enabled, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data retention and deletion requests

Information may be kept for as long as needed to provide app features, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable law. To ask about your information or request deletion, contact us using the email below. We may need enough information to identify the relevant app data and verify the request.
